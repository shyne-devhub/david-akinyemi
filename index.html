Personal project · case study

CarePoint workforce analytics

IndustryHealthcare operations TypeWorkforce & cost analysis Data12 months · 5 care homes

CarePoint is a fictional healthcare provider running five care homes. I'm exploring 12 months of workforce data on staffing levels, vacancies, overtime, agency usage and additional staffing spend. The goal is to turn raw data into insight managers can use to understand staffing pressure, monitor cost and plan better.

✓
Excel / Google SheetsPivot tables, formulas, summary tables & charts
Complete

Planned

Key business questions

  1. Which care homes have the highest additional staffing costs?
  2. How do staffing costs change over time?
  3. Which homes experience the greatest staffing shortages?
  4. Is there a relationship between vacancies and additional staffing spend?
  5. What should management investigate to improve workforce planning?
Key finding

The home with the most vacancies was not the one with the highest additional staffing cost.

Highest costOakfield House£51,716.10
Most vacanciesBrookside Care296.4 FTE

Across the five homes: £233,751 additional staffing cost and 1,222.3 FTE vacancies over 12 months.

carepoint_staffing.sqlPlanned · SQL stage
SELECT care_home,
       SUM(vacancies)        AS total_vacancies,
       SUM(extra_staff_cost) AS total_cost
FROM   carepoint_staffing
GROUP BY care_home
ORDER BY total_cost DESC;

Business value

  • More informed workforce planning
  • Better monitoring of overtime and agency spend
  • Early identification of staffing pressures
  • Evidence-based operational decisions
